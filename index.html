Save & export
One TXT file = one independent left/right pair. Nine columns: group row column left_R left_G left_B right_R right_G right_B. Import loads the visible pair. MATLAB reads/writes TXT and MAT; use TXT to exchange data with this editor.

Exports are exact 150 × 150 images, without grid lines or selection marks. Row and column numbers start at 1. Ctrl/Cmd+Z: undo. Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z: redo. Ctrl/Cmd+A: select all. Escape: clear selection. Arrow keys: move inspected pixel.
